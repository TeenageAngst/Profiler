What v0 is and is not. This desk is a transparent heuristic instrument: word, sentence, function-word, and lexicon counts computed on-device. It demonstrates the full product shape — intake, fingerprint, linkage, case file, log — with zero backend. It does not estimate IQ and its trait bars are proxies, not psychometric scores. 02 / THESIS

The case survives contact with reality.

But a narrower product emerges from the evidence than the fantasy initially suggests.

Text carries real signals about personality and cognition. Today's language models can recover some of them reliably. They cannot yet turn those signals into a standalone judgment about a person.

Core distinction

Repeatability is not truth. A model can give the same answer every time and still be measuring the wrong thing.

What holds
0.75–0.92 ICC(3,4)

Aggregating four runs produced strong reliability in a 2026 assessment-center study. Single runs were unreliable.

Härtel et al. (2026), abstract
What does not
r = 0.07–0.20

That same study found only modest agreement with trait tests and called the method insufficient for standalone selection.

Härtel et al. (2026), abstract
The stronger signal
R² ≈ 0.7

A supervised ensemble combining writing, genetic, and teacher data predicted cognitive ability near test–retest reliability. This is strong evidence for signal in writing, not proof that zero-shot LLM judgment works.

Wolfram (2025), indexed summary
The kill test

The Barnum control goes first.

People rated LLM profiles as accurate as questionnaire results even when measured convergence was only moderate. A swapped-profile control is the cheapest experiment capable of killing the central claim.

Matšenas et al. (2026), abstract
Observed distortion

The models flatter toward the social mean.

Across 32 models, agreeableness and aesthetic sensitivity ran high while negative emotionality ran low. Other work found higher agreeableness and conscientiousness and lower Machiavellianism. That is not a harmless style quirk; it is a systematic instrument bias that can make a profile feel humane while becoming less true.

PSYCH study (2026), abstract

METHOD 01

Ask items, then compute traits.

Scoring BFI-10 items before deriving traits outperformed direct “tell me their personality” prompting.

Zhu et al. (2025)
METHOD 02

Measure spread. Ignore swagger.

RLHF rewards confidence regardless of correctness. Use run-to-run and cross-model dispersion, not verbalized certainty.

Calibration review (2026)
METHOD 03

Respect the capability floor.

Instruction-tuned 7–8B systems were inconsistent or poor on several traits; external validity for extraversion, openness, and agreeableness appeared only at 62B+ in one framework.

Serapio-García et al. (2025)
Open technical question

The observed gradient — sub-5B-active models running hot by roughly 10–15 points, a 14B reasoning model centering correctly but hedging wide — fits the size-scaling literature, but remains an internal observation. Test it on a new author and a non-reasoning 14B model before calling it general.

05 / CONTROL

The boundaries are the moat.

Cross one of these lines and the instrument becomes a liability machine. Dynamic Solutions style is a shape, not a permission slip.

Build inside this box

  • Public text or text supplied with adult consent
  • Local-first inference, with no frontier API as the primary engine
  • Empirical uncertainty from at least four runs
  • Published failures, not only attractive hits
  • Consumer self-audit and consensual research

Never build this

  • Hiring, firing, promotion, tenant, credit, or insurance decisions
  • Law-enforcement risk scoring or predictive policing
  • Voice, face, video, or other biometric inference
  • Undercover personas, infiltration, or deceptive accounts
  • Marketing that promises a correct verdict about the customer
Employment

Vendor liability is live.

Mobley v. Workday allowed direct vendor liability under an agent theory; the class was certified in May 2025. A disclaimer is not armor if actual use is known.

Litigation summary, indexed
EU

Text is not biometric emotion inference.

The EU workplace and education ban applies to emotion inference from biometric data. Text-only analysis sits outside that specific prohibition, while employment use remains high-risk and GDPR still treats inferred personality as personal data.

Article 5 summary, fetched
Platform

Cloud policy can erase the engine overnight.

OpenAI's policy prohibits profiling or classifying individuals based on social behavior or personal traits as written. Local models are the stable base.

OpenAI usage policies, indexed
Mobile

Third-party AI adds consent friction.

Apple's Nov 2025 guidelines require disclosure and explicit permission before personal data is shared with a named third-party AI provider. Local inference avoids that specific transfer.
