What v0 is and is not. This desk is a transparent heuristic instrument: word, sentence, function-word, and lexicon counts computed on-device. It demonstrates the full product shape — intake, fingerprint, linkage, case file, log — with zero backend. It does not estimate IQ and its trait bars are proxies, not psychometric scores. The validated method from the research below (item-first prompting, N≥4 runs, measured spread) is what the local-model engine in v1 is for. If the bars ever look more certain than the sample deserves, the sample-confidence readout is the honest number.
